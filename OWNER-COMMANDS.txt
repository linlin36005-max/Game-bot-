OWNER COMMANDS

/admin
Shows owner commands.

/result 6:30 PM 57
Sets the actual number for the round and immediately shows the result in the current chat.

/addtime 6:30 PM
Adds a round. Round times support hour or half-hour only (00 or 30).

/removetime 6:30 PM
Removes a round.

/give 500
Reply to a member message, then use /give 500 to give virtual Coins.

/announce TEXT
Sends an announcement in the current chat.

Time format: 12-hour clock only, 1-12 + AM/PM.
Rounds can be set at :00 or :30.
